# Selara success story collection

Internal guide for gathering beta user stories for the marketing site (`/stories`).

## Interview prompts

1. What was breaking in your week before Selara — calendar, mail, follow-ups, or coordination?
2. Walk me through one specific moment where a visible plan mattered. What would have gone wrong without it?
3. What did you approve, edit, or reject? How did rejection feel compared to other tools?
4. Roughly how much time came back — per day, per week, or during a crunch period?
5. Would you recommend Selara to a peer in your field? What would you warn them about?

## Permission

- Get explicit written permission before publishing name, photo, firm, or identifiable details.
- Default to anonymized persona labels (e.g. "Litigation partner", "Clinical department leader") unless the user opts in to full attribution.
- Allow quote approval via email — send the draft quote and results list for sign-off.

## Photo guidelines

- Prefer calm, professional context — desk, phone in hand, not staged "AI hype" imagery.
- No client documents, patient identifiers, or sensitive screen content in frame.
- Offer illustration or no photo rather than a low-quality crop.

## Anonymization rules

- Remove firm names, city-specific details, and matter identifiers.
- Keep industry and role general enough that the subject cannot be identified by outsiders.
- When in doubt, shorten the quote and add "Anonymized beta example" on the published page.

## Publishing checklist

- [ ] Permission on file (email or signed form)
- [ ] Quote approved verbatim
- [ ] Anonymization review complete
- [ ] Internal links to relevant product pages (`/calendar-assistant`, `/voice-assistant`, etc.)
- [ ] Added to `lib/case-studies.ts` with slug, persona, problem, solution, results, quote
